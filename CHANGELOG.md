## 1.1.0 — not yet published

Ready to submit once accounts and Pro are live (TODO.md §1); use the store copy in `store/pro-launch.md`.

- Optional Web3Lens account: Settings → Account → Sign in with Google (a code you approve on web3lens.com). Firefox
  asks for consent to the account data at sign-in. Everything works without an account, as before.
- Pro: saved addresses sync with your account and the website; larger batches with balances through the server; JSON
  export of batches; watch/unwatch addresses and see "Watching" badges; unread watchlist alerts on the toolbar icon.
- Plan limits are shown in place (batch size, saved items) with a quiet "See Pro" link; nothing pops up.
- New strings in all 15 languages. No new permissions, no content scripts, no remote code.
