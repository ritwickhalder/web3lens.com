You open a new tab dozens of times a day. For most people it shows a search box and a few recently visited sites. If you follow crypto, that is a wasted launch pad: the first thing you do is usually check a price, open a block explorer, look up an address or read the news. This guide shows how to turn your new tab into a focused crypto research dashboard with [CentPage](https://www.centpage.com/), a free productivity new tab for Chrome, Firefox and Edge, and how to make Web3Lens part of it.

## What a research dashboard should do

A good crypto dashboard saves the clicks you repeat every day:

- **Jump to the right explorer** for each chain without searching for it.
- **Search several engines** from one box, with web, image, video and news modes.
- **Keep notes** on the case you are working on.
- **Hold your tools** such as converters, timers and calculators within reach.
- **Stay calm.** A dashboard should reduce noise, not add price tickers that tempt you to trade.

CentPage covers the layout and the tools. Web3Lens covers the blockchain-specific lookups.

## What CentPage puts on a new tab

CentPage replaces your new tab with a configurable page:

- **Multi-engine search.** Search Google, Bing and other engines from one bar, with web, image, video and news modes.
- **Curated website categories.** Ready-made tables of popular sites in many categories, so the page is useful before you customise it.
- **Custom bookmark tables.** Your own named tables of links, such as "Explorers", "Research" and "Security".
- **Widgets.** Dozens of small tools in groups, from productivity and learning to finance, health and utilities.

## Build your dashboard in six steps

### 1. Create an "Explorers" table

Make a custom table of the block explorers you use most. Not sure which ones to choose? [How to find the right block explorer](/blog/how-to-find-the-right-block-explorer) explains how to match a chain to its explorer. A starting set:

- Etherscan for Ethereum
- A Base explorer for Base
- A Bitcoin explorer such as mempool.space
- A Solana explorer for Solana
- [Web3Lens](/explorer), which routes to the right explorer for 700+ networks

Add the Web3Lens [explorer directory](/explorer) as well. It is a quick way to reach a chain you use rarely.

### 2. Add a "Research" table

Collect the sources you return to: documentation sites, governance forums, audit repositories, reputable data dashboards and the archives you use for due diligence. Keep it short. Five trusted sources beat fifty bookmarks.

### 3. Add a "Security" table

Put your verification tools in one place. Link to Web3Lens's [risk checks](/blog/blockchain-address-risk-checks), to your wallet's approvals page and to a revocation tool. When you are about to send funds, being one click from a checker makes the careful path the easy path.

### 4. Pick widgets that help, not tempt

Useful ones for research work:

- **To-do list** for the current investigation's open questions.
- **Sticky notes** for addresses, hashes and findings as you go.
- **Pomodoro timer** to keep research sessions bounded.
- **Calculator and converters** for quick arithmetic.
- **Calendar** for unlock dates, votes and deadlines.

Skip live price tickers unless you genuinely need them. Constant price movement is a distraction when your task is verification.

### 5. Install Web3Lens next to it

CentPage is your launch pad, and Web3Lens does the lookups. Together they cover most of the day:

- Press **Alt+Shift+W** for the Web3Lens popup from any tab.
- Type **`w3` and a space** in the address bar for detection-aware suggestions.
- **Right-click any selected address or hash** and choose "Look up on Web3Lens". See [browser context-menu lookups](/blog/browser-context-menu-blockchain-lookups).

### 6. Save your setup

CentPage keeps your layout in your browser. For anything important, such as a long list of custom links, export or note it down so a new browser profile does not start from zero.

> [!NOTE]
> Treat sticky notes as a scratchpad, not a vault. Never store seed phrases, private keys or exchange passwords in any browser extension, including a new-tab page.

## Privacy and permissions

A new-tab extension sees every new tab you open, so choose carefully and read the permissions. Check CentPage's listing and privacy policy on [centpage.com](https://www.centpage.com/) before installing, and keep your extension list short. Web3Lens's approach is to ask for as little as possible: no host permissions, no content scripts and no wallet connection, as explained in the [extension privacy guide](/blog/web3lens-browser-extension-features-privacy).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a new tab dashboard useful for crypto research?

Yes, when it is set up for verification rather than speculation. One-click access to the right explorers, a place to keep notes and a short list of trusted sources saves time and reduces mistakes. Leave out price tickers if they pull you toward trading.

### Can I use CentPage and Web3Lens together?

Yes. They are separate extensions with no dependency on each other. CentPage provides the page and its widgets, and Web3Lens adds address detection, risk checks and explorer routing through its popup, right-click menu and address-bar keyword.

### Is it safe to keep addresses in sticky notes?

Public addresses are fine for working notes, but be aware they can link you to your activity if someone sees your screen. Never keep recovery phrases, private keys or passwords there.

### Is CentPage free?

There is a free version with an optional Premium tier. See [centpage.com](https://www.centpage.com/) for what each includes.
